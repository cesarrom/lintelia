## NOT made
- L10 client app (10-client-app): the d11 spec was not written. The studio template text contains invented rate copy that had to be neutralised, and the spec needed new UI exploration; it is first in the plan's cut order. Budget was not the reason (0.84 USD new spend of 1.42 ceiling).
- Stretch S5 to S11: no headroom taken, to keep the first deliverable reliable.
- S12 Dealer finance end to end: too large, five simulated systems.
- X1 embedded client app, X2 API clients and webhooks, X3 integrations, X4 SSO and SCIM, X5 support access, X6 security assurance, X7 reminders and AML screening: weak picture, secrets or no business outcome; see PLAN.md section 2.3 for reasons.
- Old d03 loan reviewer decision: retired; L04 covers human review.
- All API-only specs: nothing to film; no claim made on screen or in narration that they would have to back.
